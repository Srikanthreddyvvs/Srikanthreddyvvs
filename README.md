### 👋 About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from **Vellore Institute of Technology, Amaravati** (B.Tech, CGPA 8.48/10, graduating June 2026), focused on backend engineering — building systems that stay correct and fast under load. I like working close to the metal of distributed systems: caching layers, message queues, rate limiters, and the small architectural decisions that decide whether something survives production traffic.

Outside of coursework, I build things end to end — designing the system, writing the tests, containerizing it, and shipping it through CI/CD.

- 🔭 Currently building distributed backend tooling with **Spring Boot, Kafka, and Redis**
- 🌱 Exploring **Generative AI** integrations in developer tooling (RAG pipelines with Spring AI)
- 💬 Ask me about rate limiting, caching strategies, or event-driven architecture
- 📄 Research: *Optimizing Water Consumption in Crop Production Using Clustering and ML* — presented at ICSCNA 2024
